Twin Forecast

Pick two places and one moment in the next 16 days. Temperature, humidity and wind are read from Open-Meteo and set next to each other.

      Same moment for both places, matched to the nearest hourly forecast
      Choose two places and a time, then press Compare.

      You choose the time in your browser's time zone. Each forecast is shown in its own place's local time, with the zone next to it. Wind direction is the direction the wind blows from.

      Weather data by Open-Meteo.com, licensed CC BY 4.0. Place search uses the Open-Meteo Geocoding API, which draws on GeoNames.
